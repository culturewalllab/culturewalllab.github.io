FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Do you install?

We don't send installers. Every order ships with numbered parts, a full-size paper template and mounting hardware, and a step-by-step guide, so your contractor or a local handyman can install it.

How long does it take?

Design concepts within 24 hours. Production and shipping time depend on size and materials, and we confirm them with your quote.

Can you work with our brand guidelines?

Yes. Send us your logo files, colours and fonts and we design around them.

Send us a photo of your wall.

Tell us roughly how big it is and what your company does. We'll reply with 2–3 free design concepts within 24 hours, with no obligation.
